IfWhen: Emergency Binder

Give your family a map, not a mystery.

IfWhen is the emergency binder that lives on your iPhone. It walks you through everything your loved ones would need: bank accounts, insurance, bills, documents, digital accounts, pets and final wishes.

Private by design. No account, no sign-up and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone, behind Face ID.
  • 13 guided sections with ready-made templates
  • A checklist and Readiness Score that show what to do next
  • A printable PDF binder and encrypted handoff files with IfWhen Pro

IfWhen is an organizer, not a legal service. It does not create a will or any legally binding document.
