Support

IfWhen: Emergency Binder

Need help? Email waruna@handheldworks.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Purchases

  • Restore a purchase. Open Settings in IfWhen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought Pro.
  • Cancel a subscription. On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Refunds. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • Everything you write stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so we cannot see, restore or recover your binder.
  • To keep a copy, create an encrypted handoff file from the Share tab and store it somewhere safe.
  • Deleting the app deletes the binder on that iPhone.

Handoff files

  • A handoff file opens only in IfWhen, with the passphrase you chose when you created it.
  • We cannot recover a lost passphrase. Create a new handoff file with a new passphrase instead.
  • Importing a file adds to the binder on that iPhone. It never deletes what is already there.

App lock

If Face ID does not unlock IfWhen, tap the lock screen to try again or use your iPhone passcode.
