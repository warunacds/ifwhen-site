Privacy Policy

IfWhen: Emergency Binder · Effective October 3, 2026

The short version: we collect no data. Everything you write in IfWhen stays on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing. IfWhen does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no user account, and no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software in the app.

Where your information lives

  • All entries, notes, checklist progress and attachments are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.
  • Files are protected by iOS Data Protection, which encrypts them at rest using a key tied to your device passcode. IfWhen can additionally require Face ID or your passcode every time it opens.
  • You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Face ID data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave and is never available to us.
  • If you delete the app or erase your device, your IfWhen data is deleted too. We cannot recover it, because we never had it. Use an encrypted handoff file to keep a backup.

Exports you choose to make

  • PDF binder: created on your device. It is not encrypted; it goes wherever you send, save or print it.
  • Encrypted handoff file (.ifwhen): created on your device and encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from a passphrase you choose. We never see the file or the passphrase, and we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Share the passphrase separately from the file.
  • Exports are shared only through the iOS share sheet when you ask. Once shared, the destination (for example, Mail, Messages or iCloud Drive) handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Camera, photos and files

The camera is used only to scan documents you decide to attach. Photos and files are read only when you pick them. Nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

Review reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information under Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregated sales reports from Apple — never your name, email or payment details.

Children

IfWhen is not directed to children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Because we collect no data, changes will never apply retroactively to information we hold — we hold none.
